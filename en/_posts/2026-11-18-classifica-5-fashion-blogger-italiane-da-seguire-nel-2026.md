<p>Italian fashion bloggers were born when social media was something else: personal blogs, outfit photos and a desire to tell fashion in your own voice. Today that world has moved to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, but some names remain a point of reference. This is the 2026 edition of our ranking, which updates <a href="{{ '/en/rankings/ranking-the-5-italian-fashion-bloggers-to-follow-in-2023/' | relative_url }}">the 2023 edition</a>: here are 5 Italian fashion bloggers to follow for style inspiration, trends and outfit ideas.</p>
<p>You won't find follower counts here, because they change every day and are best checked on official profiles. What matters is who they are, what they share and why they are worth following.</p>
<ol>
<li>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Chiara_Ferragni"></span>Chiara Ferragni<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><strong>Chiara Ferragni</strong>, born in Cremona in 1987, is the best-known Italian fashion blogger in the world. It all started in 2009 with the blog <strong><em>The Blonde Salad</em></strong>, which in a few years became a business: collaborations with major fashion houses, a brand in her name and a front-row seat at international shows.</p>
<p>2026 is a year of restart. In January she was acquitted in the aggravated fraud trial linked to the Pink Christmas pandoro and Easter eggs case, and shortly after she returned to fashion as the face of Guess's Spring-Summer 2026 campaign, almost thirteen years after her first collaboration with the brand. The accounts of her companies point to a phase of reorganisation and cost cutting.</p>
<p><strong>Why follow her:</strong> she is the ultimate case study in how a personal brand is built online, including the falls and the comeback.</p>
</li>
<li>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Veronica_Ferraro"></span>Veronica Ferraro<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><strong>Veronica Ferraro</strong> is the founder of <strong><em>The Fashion Fruit</em></strong>, the blog she launched in 2010 and which remains among the most followed in Italy. She doesn't only talk about fashion: her content also covers travel, fitness and wellness, with an everyday style that is easy to copy.</p>
<p>She has worked with big names in the industry and lent her face to social projects. In 2021, for example, she took part in a UNICEF campaign on children's education.</p>
<p><strong>Why follow her:</strong> if you are looking for practical everyday looks and travel ideas matched with outfits.</p>
</li>
<li>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Irene_Colzi"></span>Irene Colzi<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><strong>Irene Colzi</strong>, from Florence, with a degree in Economics, opened <strong><em>Irene's Closet</em></strong> in October 2009 and is considered one of the veterans of Italian fashion blogs. From blogger she became a YouTuber and influencer, sharing style, beauty and lifestyle through videos and tutorials. She has worked with names such as Donna Moderna, OVS and Samsung and was a fashion tutor on Detto Fatto in the 2015/16 season.</p>
<p><strong>Why follow her:</strong> for a personal, recognisable style built over more than fifteen years of work, and for her style and beauty tips in video.</p>
</li>
<li>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Eleonora_Carisi"></span>Eleonora Carisi<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><strong>Eleonora Carisi</strong>, from Turin, turned a passion for fashion born in the family shop into an entrepreneurial path: from the boutique <strong>You You Store</strong> to the line <strong>What's Inside You</strong>, up to the blog <strong><em>Jou Jou Villeroy</em></strong>, launched in 2010. She stood out for her mix of style advice and personal storytelling, with an eye on street style.</p>
<p>Today she is also founder and creative director of <strong>Grumblecreative</strong>, a platform dedicated to creativity.</p>
<p><strong>Why follow her:</strong> for anyone who wants to understand how a passion for fashion becomes a job, and for everyday urban looks.</p>
</li>
<li>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Chiara_Biasi"></span>Chiara Biasi<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p><strong>Chiara Biasi</strong>, born in Pordenone in 1990, opened her blog in 2012, after starting out as a model and hostess. Her style is glamorous and very feminine, and over time she has combined fashion, beauty and lifestyle. She told her story in the book <em>Chiaroscuri</em> and launched the swimwear line <strong>Poisson d'amour</strong> together with Pin Up Stars.</p>
<p><strong>Why follow her:</strong> for summer look ideas, beachwear and beauty, in a direct and always polished tone.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Frequently_asked_questions_about_Italian_fashion_bloggers"></span>Frequently asked questions about Italian fashion bloggers<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<h3>Who is the most famous Italian fashion blogger?</h3>
<p>Chiara Ferragni, founder of The Blonde Salad in 2009, is the best-known Italian fashion blogger, including abroad.</p>
<h3>What was the first Italian fashion blog?</h3>
<p>Among the first Italian fashion blogs are Irene's Closet and The Blonde Salad, both launched in 2009, followed by The Fashion Fruit and Jou Jou Villeroy in 2010.</p>
<h3>What is the difference between a fashion blogger and an influencer?</h3>
<p>A blogger starts from a website or personal blog, where they write and show their looks. An influencer works mainly on social media and video. Today many creators do both.</p>
<h3>How do you become a fashion blogger?</h3>
<p>You need a recognisable style, consistency in posting and collaborations with brands. The most common path is to start from a social profile or a blog, pick a specific theme and grow a loyal audience before looking for collaborations.</p>
